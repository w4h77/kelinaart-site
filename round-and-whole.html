Game Design · Two-Player Co-Op

Round & Whole

A two-player cooperative game about reuniting with someone you’ve grown apart from.

The Game

Sharing one physical ball, a parent and child squeeze, tap and hold it together to shape a tangyuan — reaching toward each other, making contact, and finally letting go.

How It Plays

No buttons and no on-screen text. Both players share a single handmade, pressure-sensing controller, and every level is cleared with one gesture made together.

What I Did

Game design, narrative, hand-drawn art and animation, Unity development and the physical controller prototype. Built with Unity, Procreate and Arduino.
