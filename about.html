Hi, I’m Kelina — Sihan Wei.

I’m an illustrator studying at the Maryland Institute College of Art, majoring in Illustration with a minor in Sequential Art. I draw animals, landscapes and organic worlds, and I also design games — building the worlds, characters and creatures that live inside them. Whether it’s a picture book or a planet, I try to give each piece a quiet, emotional story.

Most of my work starts with observation and sketching in colored pencil, graphite and technical pen, where I focus on texture and detail. From there I move into digital, building form and atmosphere with expressive line and natural color, and my studies in animation and storyboarding help me think about how an image moves and tells a story. In my game projects, like Vael, I carry the same approach into worldbuilding, character, creature and environment design. I’m working toward a freelance practice and, one day, a studio of my own.
